Starting Over - Twenty Again
============================

Every professional I know uses AI today.  We fall into two camps:

1.  Excited as hell
2.  Concerned about our future

I'm excited as hell, and I think that you should be too, but I totally
understand being concerned.  It's not clear that our job will exist next year.
It's not clear that we will still have marketable skills.  We have no idea
what we're going to do with our lives.

This is OK!  We've been through this before!  This is life in our twenties.

When you're Twenty
------

When you're twenty you're new to the workplace.  You don't have any skills or
opinions to speak of, so you go about quickly getting some.  You explore.  You
do internships.  You jump between jobs.  You try new things.  In short, you learn.

You're starting from scratch, which is painful and scary and humbling, but the
world is wide open and you can do anything, which can be kinda fun.

When you're Forty
-----

*Usually* as you age you learn hard-won lessons and develop expertise.
You translate that expertise into a financially rewarding and stable job where
you pump out productivity for lots of money.  You stop exploring and settle
into a safe and satisfying career.

We explored.  We learned.  Now we get to sit back and reap the fruits of a mature career.  Right?

Now you're Twenty Again
------------

That's what was promised, but that's not what we got.
No, our generation gets to be twenty twice!  This time with more grey hair!
That's maybe OK though! It's a really interesting time to be exploratory.

Remember that we're not competing with any senior professionals (everyone
turned twenty at the same time) so no one knows what they're doing any better
than you and we all get to reinvent ourselves and the world together.

Advice
------

Your job may not exist in a few years.

You have two choices:

-  **Operate as normal:** I think that you'll have a pretty hard time.  Maybe don't do this.
-  **Explore:** Learn to use AI in your job.  Look at getting a new job.  Learn new things.

Exploring is scary.  I get it.  Now we have kids/mortgage/whatever.
Exploring is a risky option and we want to be safe.
Sadly, there is no safe option today and you need to figure out which is less
dangerous, exploring, or sitting still.

If you sit around and operate as normal then my guess is that you'll have a hard time in a couple years.

Instead, if you act like you're twenty and explore then maybe things work out.  If it's encouraging, exploring is what those of us who are *excited as hell* are doing, and we're having a blast.

Taste
-----

I've given this spiel a few times, and I've gotten a response that rhymes with
the following surprisingly often:

> "Yeah, and we're still valuable because we developed taste over years"

To which my response is

> At least half of my "taste" I developed over the last year

There's *a lot* to learn.  Now's the time to learn it because no one knows anything
yet, and we're all twenty together.
